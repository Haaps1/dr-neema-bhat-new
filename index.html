About

A decade of dedicated hematology-oncology care

Dr. Neema Bhat is a Haemato-Oncologist based in Bangalore, with more than ten years of specialised experience treating blood disorders and blood cancers in both children and adults. She completed her MD in the United States, followed by a fellowship (FAAP) in Pediatric Hematology, Oncology & Bone Marrow Transplantation at Penn State Health — training that shapes how she diagnoses, stages and treats every patient who comes to her.

She is known for a patient-centric approach that prioritises clear communication and treatment plans built around each individual’s needs, rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol.

Treatments

Best Hematologist in Bangalore

Nine specialist treatments across adult hematology and pediatric hemato-oncology.

Hematology

  1. 01

    Anaemia Treatment

    Iron-deficiency, nutritional and inherited anaemias — finding the cause, not just treating the count.

  2. 02

    Thalassemia Treatment

    Transfusion programmes, iron chelation and evaluation for curative transplant.

  3. 03

    Hemophilia & Bleeding Disorders

    Diagnosis and long-term management of hemophilia, von Willebrand disease and low platelets.

  4. 04

    Leukaemia (Blood Cancer)

    Acute and chronic leukaemias, with chemotherapy, targeted therapy and transplant planning.

  5. 05

    Lymphoma & Myeloma

    Accurate subtyping and tailored treatment for Hodgkin, non-Hodgkin lymphoma and myeloma.

Pediatric

  1. 06

    Childhood Leukaemia & Lymphoma

    Child-centred treatment with close attention to comfort, growth and schooling.

  2. 07

    Pediatric Solid Tumours

    Neuroblastoma, Wilms tumour, sarcomas and more, with a multidisciplinary team.

  3. 08

    Pediatric Blood Disorders

    Sickle cell disease, aplastic anaemia, ITP and immune disorders in children.

  4. 09

    Bone Marrow Transplant

    Autologous and allogeneic transplants for children and adults, from donor matching to follow-up.
